THE MATERIAL

Lightweight by nature. Powerful by design.

Zephyr is positioned as a highly porous and ultra-lightweight insulation material with exceptional thermal insulating properties.

Its lightweight and flexible characteristics allow the material to be considered for applications where thermal protection must be integrated without significant additional weight.
