FIELD PROOF

From material development to deployment.

Vishwa Hara Chakra's construction-material work is built around the development of cement-free and lower-impact alternatives using recovered material streams.

The project record presented in the supplied company deck includes municipal, redevelopment, school, residential, commercial and landscaping applications, alongside infrastructure and defence-related work.

06 — PARTNER VALUE

What the system is designed to deliver.

01

Financial efficiency

The deck positions the system around a potential price advantage compared with plastic and conventional paving, while maintaining production efficiency.

02

Scalable production

The presented production model includes pavers, bricks and construction blocks, with stated daily production capacities.

03

Resource recovery

The material pathway is designed around C&D waste recovery and the development of cement-free construction products.

04

Environmental pathway

The deck describes zero cement and zero plastic use for the presented product pathway and positions it as a route toward lower-carbon construction.
