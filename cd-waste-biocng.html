SYSTEM PRINCIPLE

Two waste streams. Two pathways.

Construction and demolition waste is primarily a material-recovery stream: segregation, processing and recycling can recover useful construction materials.

Bio-CNG follows a different biological pathway and depends on suitable organic or biomass feedstock that can undergo anaerobic digestion and subsequent biogas upgrading.

C&D / 01
MATERIAL RECOVERY

Construction & Demolition Waste

C&D waste can include materials generated from construction, renovation, repair, remodeling and demolition activities. The objective of a circular approach is to preserve the recoverable value within these material streams.

01

Segregation

Separate material streams before further processing.

02

Crushing

Process suitable mineral fractions into usable sizes.

03

Screening

Grade processed materials into different fractions.

04

Recovery

Return suitable recovered materials into productive use.

MATERIAL STREAMS

What can be recovered?

01

Concrete

Mineral fractions can be processed for suitable recycled aggregate applications.

02

Bricks

Brick and masonry fractions can be separated and directed toward appropriate reuse or recycling.

03

Metals

Recoverable metals can be separated from mixed C&D material streams.

04

Soil & Fines

Soil and fine fractions can be separated through appropriate screening processes.

RENEWABLE ENERGY / 02

Organic waste to Bio-CNG.

Suitable organic and biomass waste can be processed through anaerobic digestion to produce biogas. The biogas can then be cleaned and upgraded into a methane-rich renewable gas suitable for applications supported by the project and relevant standards.

BIO-CNG PATHWAY

From organic waste to fuel.

01

Feedstock

Suitable organic and biomass waste enters the biological processing pathway.

02

Anaerobic Digestion

Microbial activity breaks down suitable organic material and generates biogas.

03

Gas Upgrading

Biogas is treated and upgraded to increase methane concentration and meet the intended specification.

04

Bio-CNG

The resulting compressed renewable gas can be directed toward suitable energy applications.
